This applies to educational content assets, including:
- lesson text,

- curriculum metadata fields authored by contributors.

This does not replace the software license for code.

## Attribution requirements
When reusing or adapting curriculum content, include:
- project name: NOL Math,
- repository URL,
- indication of modifications,
- CC BY-SA 4.0 notice and link.

## ShareAlike requirement
Adapted content distributed publicly must be shared under the same or a compatible ShareAlike license as required by CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Contributor agreement for content PRs
By submitting content to `curriculum/`, contributors confirm that:
- they own the content or have rights to contribute it,
- contribution does not knowingly infringe third-party rights,
- content is provided under CC BY-SA 4.0,
- they understand content may be modified by other contributors.

## Third-party materials
If a contribution includes third-party material, the contributor must document source and license compatibility in the PR.

## Conflict rule
If a file includes a specific license header, that header takes precedence for that file.
